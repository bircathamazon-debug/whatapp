# Panama: country profile for an AI WhatsApp/IVR appointment-booking SaaS (as of Oct 2026)

## 1. How many small and appointment-based businesses exist?

### Takeaway
Panama has no clean official count of salons, barbershops or clinics. The total number of MSMEs ranges from about 25k (formal, older figure) to about 200k (including informal), and about 70% of micro and small firms are informal. The best indicators of size are about 100k beauty-sector workers (2019, 93% informal) and about 8,000 doctors plus 1,300 dentists (2025, INEC). The realistic formal, digitally reachable target is probably a few thousand businesses, mostly in Panama City.

### Cited Findings
- MSMEs make up 92% of Panama's business structure. AMPYME's administrator Raúl Fernández said on 30 Aug 2024 that "70% of micro and small businesses operate informally". CCIAP estimates about 700k informal people, and Ceecam puts 47.4% of the employed population in informality — [La Estrella, 30 Aug 2024](https://www.laestrella.com.pa/economia/ampyme-el-70-de-las-microempresas-en-panama-operan-bajo-la-informalidad-DI8390126)
- One source counts "more than 200,000 MSMEs = 97% of companies" (Alegra.com data, 2022) — [DPL News](https://dplnews.com/panama-mas-del-60-de-las-mipymes-son-dirigidas-por-mujeres/). Panamá América says "200 mil empresas Mype, sin contar las agrícolas" (undated) — [Panamá América](https://www.panamaamerica.com.pa/economia/crean-plataforma-de-inclusion-para-impulsar-microempresas-919497)
- Contradicting those: "official figures indicate there are 25 thousand MSMEs" (article undated, likely older; probably formal firms only) — [La Prensa](https://www.prensa.com/economia/dan-primeros-pasos-para-facilitar-tramites-emprendedores-en-panama/). The Cámara de Comercio's Garage Digital program said "almost 9 thousand registered companies in Panama are SMEs" — [DPL News](https://dplnews.com/capacitaran-digitalmente-a-la-pequena-y-mediana-empresa-panamena/)
- AMPYME's 2025 database "exceeds 600,000 MSME leaders/entrepreneurs". This counts registered entrepreneurs, not active businesses. About 5,000 businesses were formalized in 2025 — [La Estrella](https://www.laestrella.com.pa/economia/ecosistema-de-emprendedores-en-panama-crece-a-mas-de-600000-DM18362405)
- MSME sales fell 15.54% in the first half of 2024 (Alegra data) — [La Estrella](https://www.laestrella.com.pa/economia/mipymes-de-panama-reportan-caidas-en-ventas-durante-el-primer-semestre-de-2024-AD7764604)
- Beauty sector: INEC data (cited by René Quevedo) shows 100,511 workers in "salones de belleza" in August 2019, 93% informal. Nine in ten work informally or in firms with 10 or fewer staff. The workforce is 52% women and 48% men. The salon owners' association (Asociación de Dueños de Salones de Belleza y Barberías) had only about 25 members. No count of establishments was given (article dated 9 Jun 2020) — [La Estrella, 2020](https://www.laestrella.com.pa/economia/200606-restricciones-afectan-peluquerias-HPLE427563)
- Economic census: the VII Censo Nacional Económico ran from 2 Jun to 31 Dec 2025 and covered more than 39,000 establishments. Preliminary results were planned for Mar 2026 and final results for Jul 2026. I found no published results — [La Prensa](https://www.prensa.com/economia/panama-inicia-un-censo-economico-con-miras-a-fortalecer-la-planificacion-e-inversion/); [Telemetro](https://www.telemetro.com/nacionales/contraloria-inicia-vii-censos-nacionales-economicos-el-pais-el-31-diciembre-n6041934). An INEC commentary on the directory update mentions "79,918 units located" (from a search snippet only, not verified) — [INEC](https://www.inec.gob.pa/archivos/P07055475202411221003425_Comentario.pdf)
- Health: INEC counts 7,515 doctors and 1,239 dentists in 2024 (provisional). Of these, 3,991 doctors and 528 dentists are in Panamá province. The 2025 figures are 8,068 doctors and 1,325 dentists (provisional). These count staff in health facilities, not private practices — [INEC 2024](https://www.inec.gob.pa/archivos/A057951862025072910103803%20M%C3%89DICOS,%20ODONT%C3%93LOGOS,%20ENFERMEROS%202024.pdf); [INEC 2025](https://www.inec.gob.pa/archivos/A05334242026073008573703%20M%C3%89DICOS,%20ODONT%C3%93LOGOS,%20ENFERMEROS%202025.pdf)
- Fresha lists 49 "hair salons" in Panama (a mix of salons, nail spas and barbershops), with 11,060 reviews across 35 venues. This is a small, digitally advanced, upscale slice of the market — [Fresha Panama](https://www.fresha.com/lp/en/bt/hair-salons/in/panama)

### Inferences
- With about 100k beauty workers, 90%+ of them informal or in micro firms, there are likely thousands to low tens of thousands of beauty points of sale (including home-based ones). Only a minority are formal and able to pay a USD subscription by card. This is an order-of-magnitude estimate, not a sourced figure.
- Medical and dental: about 8k doctors and 1.3k dentists. Many work in the public system (CSS/MINSA). The private office segment is likely low thousands of practices, concentrated in Panama City (Panamá province has about 53% of doctors).
- A realistic serviceable market for a paid SaaS is probably about 2,000–6,000 businesses. This is an inference, so treat it as a rough range.

### Gaps
- No official count of beauty salons, barbershops, nail salons, aesthetics or private clinics was found. The 2025 economic census results (due Jul 2026) were not found online. Searching inec.gob.pa by CIIU 9602 (peluquería) would be the next step.
- AMPYME's revenue-based definitions of micro and small firms were not retrieved.

## 2. Population, GDP, dollarization, purchasing power, service prices

### Takeaway
Panama is small (4.58M people) but the richest economy in Central America, at about US$17–20k GDP per capita. It uses the US dollar (the balboa is pegged 1:1), so there is no currency risk for a USD subscription. Typical prices are a US$10–22 men's haircut, US$15–35+ women's cut, and US$50–70 for a private specialist visit. A subscription of US$20–50/month is affordable for a formal salon or clinic.

### Cited Findings
- Population: 4.58M (Oct 2025, UN), growing +1.2% a year. 70.4% urban, median age 30.3 — [DataReportal Digital 2026 Panama](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2026-panama)
- GDP per capita estimates vary by source and method:
  - US$19,802 for 2025 (Cetcam study via EFE), the highest in Central America ahead of Costa Rica at US$19,104 — [Diario El Mundo](https://diario.elmundo.sv/economia/nicaragua-tiene-el-ingreso-por-habitante-mas-bajo-de-centroamerica-segun-estudio)
  - US$17,137 nominal and US$36,426 PPP for 2024 — [Trading Economics](https://es.tradingeconomics.com/panama/gdp)
  - World Bank figure for 2023: €13,807 per capita — [Spanish MFA country sheet](https://www.exteriores.gob.es/Documents/FichasPais/PANAMA_FICHA%20PAIS.pdf)
- Growth: the economy grew 4.4% in 2025 (MEF). The World Bank had forecast 3.9% — [MEF Avance Anual 2025](https://www.mef.gob.pa/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/Avance-Anual-2025-Informe-Economico-y-Social-DAES.pdf)
- Dollarization: Fresha lists Panamanian prices in balboas (PAB), which equal the US dollar — [Fresha listings](https://www.fresha.com/lp/en/bt/hair-salons/in/panama)
- Minimum wage: Decreto Ejecutivo No. 13 (31 Dec 2025) applies from 16 Jan 2026 for 2026–2027. It sets hourly rates by sector, region and firm size, for example US$2.32–3.13/h in manufacturing (Region 1) and US$3.51/h in construction (Region 1) — [La Prensa](https://www.prensa.com/economia/salario-minimo-2026-en-panama-estas-son-las-nuevas-tarifas-por-actividad-y-region/)
- Haircut prices on Fresha (Panama City, 2025–26):
  - Men's cut PAB 20 (about 30 min) at several barbershops; "Signature Haircut" PAB 35 (Frank Barber Shop); a low outlier of PAB 6.50 — [Fresha search results](https://www.fresha.com/lp/en/pa-san-miguelito)
  - Women's cut from PAB 35 (Atempo) and PAB 15 (Mr Ted); men's and kids' cuts PAB 10–22; color from PAB 50; highlights PAB 200; balayage PAB 107–300 — [Fresha Panama](https://www.fresha.com/lp/en/bt/hair-salons/in/panama)
- Medical prices:
  - A private-hospital specialist visit costs about US$50–70 (expat guide, anecdotal). Public clinics charge US$5 or less — [Panama Healthcare Guide](https://panama-work.bss.design/Blog/medical-services-in-panama.html)
  - Insurance copays in one Oct 2025 policy: specialist B/.25, GP B/.10 — [MAPFRE Panamá](https://www.mapfre.com.pa/media/FIRST-CLASS.pdf)

### Inferences
- A salon doing 10 cuts a day at US$15–20 makes about US$3–5k a month in gross revenue. A US$20–40/month tool is about 1% of revenue, and one recovered no-show (US$20–60) roughly pays for a month. Panama's price levels are the best in Central America for a USD SaaS.
- Being dollarized removes FX and inflation risk. List prices can match US/LatAm USD pricing.

### Gaps
- No official World Bank 2025 GDP per capita figure was retrieved; the figures above conflict.
- No systematic price survey exists for manicures (Fresha snippets only) or GP private consultations.

## 3. WhatsApp, internet and smartphone penetration; how bookings happen today

### Takeaway
Digital readiness is high. Internet penetration is 78.4% and social media reaches 98% of adults. WhatsApp is the dominant messaging app (no Panama-specific survey found, but regional peers show 97–99%). Bookings today happen mainly through WhatsApp chats, Instagram DMs and phone calls. Only a small upscale tier uses marketplaces like Fresha.

### Cited Findings
- Internet: 3.60M users at the end of 2025, 78.4% penetration, +12% year on year. About 989k people (21.6%) are offline — [DataReportal 2026](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2026-panama). Early 2025: 3.54M users (78.0%) — [DataReportal 2025](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2025-panama)
- Mobile: 4.85M cellular connections (106% of population), 94.6% of them broadband (3G/4G/5G). Median mobile download 32.43 Mbps, fixed 185.94 Mbps — [DataReportal 2026](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2026-panama)
- Social media:
  - 3.18M user identities (69.3% of population; equal to 98.1% of adults 18+), as of Oct 2025 — [DataReportal 2026](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2026-panama)
  - Instagram ad reach 2.70M (80.3% of adults); Facebook 2.00M (61.8% of adults) — [DataReportal 2026](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2026-panama)
- WhatsApp:
  - WMTips (2026) gives WhatsApp a 92.1% share of messaging widgets on Panamanian websites, vs Telegram 3.8%. This measures web presence, not users — [WMTips](https://www.wmtips.com/technologies/instant-messaging/country/pa/)
  - Peer countries: Costa Rica 97% WhatsApp penetration (UCR 2023) — [Semanario Universidad](https://semanariouniversidad.com/pais/21-de-ticos-admiten-compartir-desinformacion-por-whatsapp-la-plataforma-de-mas-uso-en-el-pais/); Peru 98.6% of users (Osiptel, end 2025) — [Forbes Perú](https://forbes.pe/negocios/2026-06-03/mas-del-60-de-los-peruanos-prefiere-las-billeteras-digitales-y-la-mensajeria-instantanea/)
- WhatsApp Business among SMEs: a 2021 University of Panama study of fast-food restaurants found 63% of firms using WhatsApp Business as a marketing tool found it very useful — [Revista Colón CTN, UP](https://revistas.up.ac.pa/index.php/revista_colon_ctn/article/download/2241/2075/3754). A 2025 UP study covers WhatsApp Business and consumer purchase decisions — [Revista Guacamaya, UP](https://revistas.up.ac.pa/index.php/guacamaya/article/download/7037/5355/11808)
- Fresha is actively used by top Panama City venues, for example Ondulados Panamá (3,398 reviews), Aldo's Salon (1,705) and Atempo (1,392). Some Panamanian businesses already accept online booking — [Fresha Panama](https://www.fresha.com/lp/en/bt/hair-salons/in/panama)

### Inferences
- WhatsApp is almost certainly the main booking channel for Panamanian salons, barbers and doctors' offices, together with phone calls and Instagram DMs. Combined with informality, this means the owner (or a receptionist) answers chats by hand. That is the pain point this product solves.
- An IVR is less critical in Panama than in Israel (there is no "kosher phone" segment). Phone calls still matter for older patients at medical and dental offices.

### Gaps
- No Panama-specific survey of WhatsApp penetration or WhatsApp Business adoption among SMEs was found (DataReportal 2026 Panama shows no WhatsApp figure).
- No survey quantifies how salons or clinics take bookings (paper, WhatsApp, software).
- No smartphone share figure was found.

## 4. Payments: Stripe, Yappy, ACH, PayPal; collecting monthly USD subscriptions

### Takeaway
Stripe does not support Panamanian merchants (in Latin America it lists only Brazil and Mexico). That doesn't matter for collecting from Panamanian customers: a seller with a Stripe account elsewhere (e.g. a US LLC) can bill Panamanian credit and debit cards in USD. Locally, Yappy (Banco General) is the dominant wallet, with more than 1.8M users and more than 34k merchants. PagueloFacil is the main local card gateway. Card and digital payment use is moderate: 64% of adults have an account and 52% made digital payments.

### Cited Findings
- Stripe's global availability page lists only Brazil and Mexico in Latin America. Panama is not listed — [Stripe Global](https://stripe.com/global)
- A founder guide (Apr 2026) suggests that if Panama is unavailable for direct signup, many founders form an entity in a supported country, such as a US LLC — [Zenind](https://www.zenind.com/help/post/how-to-open-a-stripe-account-from-panama-a-practical-guide-for-founders)
- Yappy:
  - Launched in 2019 by Banco General. It has more than 1.8M customers and more than 34k merchants, and is adding four more banks (reported after Feb 2025) — [La Prensa](https://www.prensa.com/economia/usuarios-reportan-fallas-para-pagar-con-yappy/)
  - 2024 volume: 487M transactions, of which 431M were person to person. Merchant operations exceeded 56M transactions, +300% vs 2023 — [La Prensa](https://www.prensa.com/economia/usuarios-reportan-fallas-para-pagar-con-yappy/)
  - Oct 2022: 19k micro-enterprises were affiliated with Yappy Comercial, with about 30k new users a month — [La Prensa, 2022](https://www.prensa.com/economia/transformacion-digital-yappy-pega-el-salto/)
- PagueloFacil is a Panamanian online gateway (credit cards, Clave debit, PagoCash, crypto) with Shopify, Odoo and Drupal plugins — [Shopify App Store](https://apps.shopify.com/paguelofacil); [Odoo](https://apps.odoo.com/apps/modules/14.0/payment_paguelofacil). PayPal is available as a default option for Panama in Shopify — [Shopify Community](https://community.shopify.com/t/pasarelas-de-pago-en-panama/144261)
- Financial inclusion (Global Findex 2025, data from 2024): 64% of adults have an account, 52% made digital payments, and 33% save formally. Panama ranks second in Central America after Costa Rica (71/60/35) — [Revista E&N, 17 Jul 2025](https://www.revistaeyn.com/finanzas/ranking-de-bancarizacion-de-los-paises-de-centroamerica-segun-el-informe-global-findex-OG26642185). An older MEF figure gave 46% account ownership (an earlier edition) — [Panamá América](https://www.panamaamerica.com.pa/economia/panamenos-sin-plata-para-abrir-cuentas-bancarias-1137792)

### Inferences
- Recommended approach: bill monthly in USD by card through a Stripe account in a supported country (US LLC or similar), so no currency conversion is needed. Offer Yappy or ACH transfer as a manual or annual-prepay alternative for owners who prefer not to use a card. Formal salon and clinic owners usually have cards; informal micro-businesses often won't.
- I found no documentation of automatic recurring billing through Yappy or PagueloFacil. Building recurring billing locally would need custom tokenization (similar to the Tranzila cron already built for Israel).

### Gaps
- No source was retrieved on Yappy merchant fees or whether Yappy has a recurring-payment API.
- No source was retrieved on PagueloFacil or Tilopay recurring tokenization.
- No figure was found for credit card penetration among SMB owners.
- The claim that a foreign Stripe account can charge Panamanian cards is my inference from how Stripe works in general. I did not confirm it with a Panama-specific source.

## 5. Data protection: Ley 81 of 2019, its regulation, and health data

### Takeaway
Panama has a GDPR-style law. Ley 81 of 2019 took effect on 29 Mar 2021, and its regulation is Decreto Ejecutivo 285 of 28 May 2021, enforced by ANTAI. It requires prior, informed, documented consent. It treats health data as sensitive. It restricts international transfers unless protection is adequate or the person consents. Fines are modest (B/.1,000–10,000). It is manageable for a SaaS: a consent text in the bot, a privacy policy, a processor contract with each business, and care with medical data.

### Cited Findings
- Ley 81 took effect on 29 Mar 2021. It applies to public and private entities that process personal data, including databases located in Panama and controllers domiciled there. Regulators are ANTAI and AIG — [RSM Panamá](https://www.rsm.global/panama/es/node/62)
- Sanctions: ANTAI fines of B/.1,000–10,000 depending on severity and repeat offenses. Other measures include warnings, closure of the database registry and suspension of processing. Controllers must also compensate material and moral damages — [RSM Panamá](https://www.rsm.global/panama/es/node/62)
- Decreto Ejecutivo 285 of 28 May 2021 sets out consent, data subject rights, international transfers and security measures. It also covers the data protection officer, the registry of databases transferred to third parties and prescription periods — [Lex Mundi](https://www.lexmundi.com/guides/data-privacy-guide/jurisdictions/latin-america/panama/); [LexLatin](https://lexlatin.com/entrevistas/reglamento-la-ley-proteccion-datos-panama)
- Sensitive data includes health, genetic and biometric data, religious beliefs, sexual orientation and more — [Kennedys](https://kennedyslaw.com/thought-leadership/article/ley-no-81-panama-becomes-latest-jurisdiction-in-the-region-to-adopt-gdpr-like-protections)
- Consent must be prior, informed and unequivocal, documented in a traceable way, and can be revoked without retroactive effect. Sensitive data may be transferred without explicit consent only to safeguard the person's life — [Kraemer Law](https://kraemerlaw.com/en/articles/law-81-of-2019-about-protection-of-personal-data-in-panama/)
- International transfers are allowed if the recipient offers comparable protection or the transferring party takes protective measures. Exceptions include consent and contract performance — [Morimor](https://morimor.com/panama-new-law-on-protection-of-personal-data/)
- Breach notification within 72 hours of discovery under the Decree (single source) — [Kennedys](https://kennedyslaw.com/thought-leadership/article/ley-no-81-panama-becomes-latest-jurisdiction-in-the-region-to-adopt-gdpr-like-protections)
- Enforcement is real but limited. ANTAI sanctioned a digital outlet over a published marriage certificate, and the case was appealed to the 9-member Consejo de Protección de Datos — [La Prensa](https://www.prensa.com/opinion/la-proteccion-de-datos-y-la-libertad-de-prensa-no-son-incompatibles/); [La Prensa](https://www.prensa.com/impresa/panorama/se-registran-las-primeras-denuncias-por-presunta-violacion-de-datos-personales/). A 2026 Órgano Judicial analysis notes structural weaknesses in the sanction regime — [Órgano Judicial, 2026](https://www.organojudicial.gob.pa/uploads/blogs.dir/28/2026/06/913/la-proteccion-de-datos-personales-en-la-republica-de-panama.pdf)

### Inferences
- Data hosted outside Panama (the current Firebase project uses me-west1 Tel Aviv / us-central1) should be fine if covered by consent and contractual safeguards. For medical clients, avoid storing clinical details (diagnoses) in booking notes, or add explicit consent.
- The regulatory risk is low to moderate and does not block sales.

### Gaps
- I did not read the official texts of Ley 81 or Decreto 285 (Gaceta Oficial). Specific health-data rules (e.g. Ley 68 of 2003 on patient rights and the clinical record) were not researched.
- The 72-hour breach-notification rule comes from a single source.

## 6. Local competitors and tools already used in Panama

### Takeaway
Global and regional booking tools are already present: Fresha (actively used by top Panama City salons and barbers), AgendaPro (has a /pa/ country site and sends WhatsApp reminders), and Booksy (Spanish-language product; no Panama-specific evidence found). I found no Panamanian startup offering AI WhatsApp booking. The competition is mostly "manual WhatsApp" plus a few marketplace apps, not a local incumbent.

### Cited Findings
- Fresha: 49 Panama "hair salon" listings plus barbers and nail spas, with 11,060 reviews across 35 venues. Top venues include Ondulados Panamá (3,398 reviews), Aldo's Salon (1,705), Atempo Hair Concept (1,392) and Capitan Barber Shop (1,034) — [Fresha Panama](https://www.fresha.com/lp/en/bt/hair-salons/in/panama)
- AgendaPro runs a Panama country page (agendapro.com/pa/). It offers a booking site, WhatsApp/SMS/email reminders, CRM, POS and multi-branch support, and markets itself as the "#1 booking system in Latin America" (a self-claim) — [AgendaPro PA](https://agendapro.com/pa/agenda-online)
- Booksy: Spanish (es-US) business site for barbers and salons, starting at about US$30/month according to a competitor comparison site. No evidence was found of a Panama-specific presence — [Booksy biz](https://biz.booksy.com/es-us/quien-nos-ama/barberos); [SchedulingKit](https://schedulingkit.com/es/alternatives/booksy)
- Regional AI WhatsApp booking players exist (e.g. kAI Lab, Mexico; AsistenteCitas, origin unknown; Aunoa, Spain). No Panamanian one was found — [descubre.vc](https://www.descubre.vc/e/kai); [Aunoa](https://aunoa.ai/producto/chatbot-de-whatsapp-para-la-gestion-de-citas)

### Inferences
- Fresha's presence shows that Panama City's upscale salons already accept online booking. The AI WhatsApp angle (no app download, natural language, voice notes) is a real differentiator against marketplace-style tools.
- Medical: no specific Panama booking competitor was identified in this research (Doctoralia's presence was not checked).

### Gaps
- Not checked: Doctoralia/Docplanner presence in Panama, local clinic software (e.g. EMR vendors with booking), Reservo, Weibook, Calendly usage.
- Market shares are unavailable. Pricing is covered by another researcher.

## 7. Is Panama big enough alone, or a beachhead for Central America?

### Takeaway
On its own Panama is a niche market: 4.58M people, a few thousand formal and reachable appointment businesses, and about 70% micro-firm informality. It could support a profitable small business (for example 300–500 paying customers × US$30 is about US$9–15k MRR), but not a large SaaS. Its strengths are dollarization, the region's highest income, banking second only to Costa Rica, and its role as a logistics and finance hub. That makes it a good beachhead or test market for Spanish-speaking Central America, especially alongside Costa Rica. The poorer, less banked markets of Guatemala, Honduras, El Salvador and Nicaragua are harder.

### Cited Findings
- Panama has the highest GDP per capita in Central America (US$19,802, 2025), ahead of Costa Rica (US$19,104), with Nicaragua lowest — [Diario El Mundo / Cetcam](https://diario.elmundo.sv/economia/nicaragua-tiene-el-ingreso-por-habitante-mas-bajo-de-centroamerica-segun-estudio)
- Account ownership by country: Costa Rica 71%, Panama 64%, El Salvador 43%, Honduras 42%, Guatemala 38%, Nicaragua 23% (Findex 2025) — [Revista E&N](https://www.revistaeyn.com/finanzas/ranking-de-bancarizacion-de-los-paises-de-centroamerica-segun-el-informe-global-findex-OG26642185)
- Panama's economy grew 4.4% in 2025 — [MEF](https://www.mef.gob.pa/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/Avance-Anual-2025-Informe-Economico-y-Social-DAES.pdf). The World Bank expects Panama to lead regional growth at 4.1% in 2026 — [Panamá América](https://www.panamaamerica.com.pa/economia/panama-encabezara-el-crecimiento-economico-de-la-region-con-41-en-2026-segun-el-banco/amp)
- Population is 4.58M, growing 1.2% a year — [DataReportal 2026](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2026-panama)

### Inferences
- Suggested feasibility score for Panama alone: about 5–6/10.
  - Positives: USD economy, high WhatsApp/Instagram use, no local AI incumbent, Spanish (already supported by the product), affordable price points, light privacy regime.
  - Negatives: small market, high informality, Stripe unavailable locally (workable via a foreign entity), Fresha and AgendaPro already present in the upscale tier, and the product's Baileys-based WhatsApp would need the official Meta API before scaling.
- Combined with Costa Rica (similar income and banking), the addressable base roughly doubles. El Salvador is also dollarized (general knowledge, not sourced here) but much less banked.
- These are judgment calls from the cited data, not sourced figures.

### Gaps
- No reliable 2025 population figures for the six Central American countries were retrieved. Macrotrends' "Central America ~183.8M" likely includes Mexico (UN region definition), so it was not used.
- No data was found on cross-border SaaS sales from Panama into the region, or on regional appointment-business counts.
