# Venezuela: market profile for an AI appointment-booking SaaS (WhatsApp bot + IVR + web panel)

Research date: 2026-10-25. Most sources are dated 2025-2026. Older data is flagged with its year. Venezuela's political situation changed sharply in 2026 (see the economic section), so anything before January 2026 is especially likely to be out of date.

## 1. How many small and appointment-based businesses exist? How big is the beauty/personal-care sector?

### Takeaway
I found no official or credible count of salons, barbershops, nail salons, aesthetics centers or private clinics in Venezuela, and no total count of SMBs. The economy is mostly informal: more than half of economic activity and about 70% of employment. Many barbers and stylists work informally, sometimes on the street. The real addressable market of formal businesses that can pay is unknown and probably small.

### Cited Findings
- No official Venezuelan count of hair salons, barbershops or beauty salons turned up. The only Venezuela-specific numbers are online directories. Venezuela Yello lists about 92 companies in its "Peluquerías y Salones de Belleza" category (91 in Caracas) and 13 in Valencia. These reflect directory sign-ups, not a census. — [Venezuela Yello: Caracas](https://www.venezuelayello.com/category/Peluquer%C3%ADas_y_Salones_de_Belleza/city:Caracas); [Venezuela Yello: Venezuela](https://www.venezuelayello.com/category/Peluquer%C3%ADas_y_Salones_de_Belleza)
- Regional benchmark only, not Venezuela: Mexico's 2019 Economic Census counted 216,466 units in "Salones y Clínicas de Belleza, Baños Públicos y Bolerías". — [DataMéxico](https://www.economia.gob.mx/datamexico/es/profile/industry/personal-care-services?redirect=true)
- Informality: IESA, using ENCOVI data, says more than half of economic activity is informal. Ecoanalítica puts labor informality at about 70%, against a regional average of about 50% (November 2024). — [Finanzas Digital / Fedecámaras forum](https://finanzasdigital.com/foro-fedecamaras-formalizacion-emprendedores-venezuela/); [Finanzas Digital](https://finanzasdigital.com/fedecamaras-informalidad-carga-tributaria/)
- Conindustria reported about 3,200 active industrial firms, with about 22% (about 700) inoperative. The article is undated in the snippet, so the year is unverified. This covers industry only, not services. — [vLex Venezuela](https://vlexvenezuela.com/vid/conindustria-calcula-dejaron-operar-744401325)
- Fedecámaras' Q1 2026 survey found 46.4% of installed capacity idle, blamed on lack of financing and weak domestic demand. — [El Nacional, June 2026](https://www.elnacional.com/2026/06/salario-privado-promedio-venezuela-encuesta-fedecamaras/); [Aragua Press](https://araguapress.com/encuesta-de-fedecamaras-revela-precariedad-salarial-y-alta-capacidad-ociosa-empresarial/)
- Street and informal barbering became common during the crisis. These reports are from 2018-2019 and only describe the trend. — [RPP](https://rpp.pe/mundo/actualidad/las-barberias-de-venezuela-se-mudaron-a-las-calles-debido-a-la-crisis-fotos-noticia-1180062); [NTN24](https://www.ntn24.com/noticias-actualidad/las-barberias-en-caracas-no-cierran-se-mudan-a-las-calles-103834)

### Inferences
- With a population of about 28.5M and heavy informality, there are probably tens of thousands of beauty and personal-care service points. Only a small share are formal, multi-chair salons that run a structured agenda and could pay a recurring subscription in USD. That segment is concentrated in eastern Caracas, Valencia, Maracaibo, Barquisimeto and Lechería.
- Private medical and dental offices are probably the most able to pay among the target verticals, but I found no count of them.

### Gaps
- No INE, SENIAT or Registro Mercantil count of businesses by activity. No figures on sector size (USD revenue) for beauty or personal care. No count of private clinics or dental offices. These sources did not appear in searches, and Venezuela has not published regular economic census data in years.

## 2. WhatsApp and digital penetration, and how businesses take bookings today

### Takeaway
About 62% of Venezuelans are online (17.6M of 28.5M, unchanged year over year), and there are 21.8M mobile connections. Social media use is growing fast: 16.6M identities, +14% in a year. WhatsApp is widely assumed to be the main messaging app, but I found no reliable Venezuela-specific user figure for it. Bookings at small businesses appear to run mostly through manual WhatsApp chats and Instagram, but this is inferred from vendor pages, not measured.

### Cited Findings
- Digital 2026 (data as of October 2025): population 28.5M (88.6% urban, median age 29.4). Internet users 17.6M (61.6%), +69K (+0.4%) year over year, adoption flat. 11.0M people (38.4%) are offline. — [DataReportal Digital 2026 Venezuela](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2026-venezuela)
- Cellular connections: 21.8M (76.3% of population), +2.7%, with 99.8% broadband (3G/4G/5G). — [DataReportal Digital 2026](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2026-venezuela)
- Speeds (Ookla, August 2025): median mobile download 25.42 Mbps (+86% year over year) and median fixed download 92.09 Mbps (+54%). — [DataReportal Digital 2026](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2026-venezuela)
- Social media: 16.6M user identities (58.1% of population; 83.8% of adults; 94.3% of internet users), +2.1M (+14.3%). — [DataReportal Digital 2026](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2026-venezuela)
- Ad reach: Facebook 15.6M (54.5%), Instagram 9.8M (34.3%), TikTok 16.6M (83.8% of adults). DataReportal does not publish WhatsApp reach for Venezuela. — [DataReportal Digital 2026](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2026-venezuela)
- Digital 2025 (start of 2025): 17.5M internet users, 61.6% penetration, 15.1M social media identities (53.1%). — [DataReportal Digital 2025](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2025-venezuela)
- A vendor blog estimates WhatsApp penetration at about 80-85% with 12-14M monthly active users, but cites no methodology. Treat this as low confidence. — [Mazkara Studio](https://mazkara.studio/en/newsletter/whatsapp-penetration-latin-america-2026/)
- Sensor Tower Q2 2025: WhatsApp held steady at about 70K weekly downloads in April 2025. These are downloads, not users. — [Sensor Tower](https://sensortower.com/blog/2025-q2-unified-top-5-communication%20apps-units-ve-6070aae1241bc16eb81f5bab)
- Meta's WhatsApp Business API pricing (July 2025) puts Venezuela in the "Rest of LATAM" tier: about $0.074 per marketing message and $0.0113 per utility or authentication message. — [Mazkara Studio](https://mazkara.studio/en/newsletter/whatsapp-penetration-latin-america-2026/)
- Booking tools marketed to Venezuelan salons and barbershops sell themselves on a "link for Instagram and WhatsApp" plus WhatsApp reminders. This suggests WhatsApp and Instagram are the default channels for booking requests. — [ReservaSimple](https://www.reservasimple.com/agendador-citas-online-venezuela); [Turnito](https://turnito.app/app-turnos-peluqueria/)

### Inferences
- WhatsApp is almost certainly the dominant booking channel. Coverage is limited by about 38% of people being offline, which matters most for low-income and rural customers.
- An IVR is less important in Venezuela than in Israel (no "kosher phone" segment). It could still help reach offline or older clients, but local telephony is a weak point. CANTV, the state landline operator, has deteriorated, and I found no data on VoIP and DID number availability (for example on Telnyx).

### Gaps
- No reliable survey of WhatsApp penetration or WhatsApp Business adoption among Venezuelan SMBs. No survey of how salons and clinics take bookings (manual WhatsApp vs. Instagram DM vs. phone). No data on whether Venezuelan phone numbers can be bought for an IVR.

## 3. Economic context 2025-2026: dollarization, purchasing power, prices, willingness to pay

### Takeaway
2026 has been a year of upheaval:
- **Political change:** the US captured Maduro on Jan 3, 2026, and Delcy Rodríguez became acting president. The US recognized her government in March and relations were restored.
- **Inflation still extreme:** official annual inflation was about 466% in September 2026, and the IMF projects about 468% for year-end. Monthly inflation was 8.4% in September.
- **Exchange-rate gap narrowing:** the official/parallel gap has fallen to roughly 12-15%.

The economy is de facto dollarized for pricing. Purchasing power is very low: the average private salary was $283/month in Q1 2026. Haircuts cost about $5-10 in Caracas (2024 data). A $15-30/month software subscription would be a meaningful expense for most small salons.

### Cited Findings
**Politics and sanctions timeline**
- Jan 3, 2026: US strikes on Caracas and capture of Maduro.
- Jan 5: Delcy Rodríguez sworn in as acting president.
- Jan 29: law opening oil to private firms.
- Mar 5: US and Venezuela agree to restore diplomatic relations; the US embassy reopens Mar 14.
- Mar 7: US formally recognizes Rodríguez's government.
- Apr 30: commercial US flights resume.
- Jul 24: US imposes a 12.5% tariff.
- Oct 9: Starlink licenses approved.

Source for the timeline: [Wikipedia: 2026 in Venezuela](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_in_Venezuela)

**Inflation and exchange rate**
- BCV: monthly inflation 8.4% in September 2026. — [BCV](https://www.bcv.org.ve/notas-de-prensa/la-inflacion-fue-de-84-en-septiembre)
- The government says monthly inflation fell from 20% (July) to 8% (September), and annualized inflation fell from 649% (March) to 466% (September). It also says the exchange gap fell from 65.7% (January 2026) to 11.7% (October). — [RNV (state media)](https://rnv.gob.ve/brecha-cambiaria-e-inflacion-desaceleracion-en-2026/)
- IMF projection for end-2026 inflation: 467.9%. — [ACN](https://acn.com.ve/crecimiento-inflacion-venezuela-cierre-2026/)
- Independent estimates of the exchange gap differ from the government's 11.7%. In August, La Patilla put it at 12-15% after the BCV sold $9.2B in FX during 2026. Economist Jesús Palacios said the gap was still high in May (about 45%). — [La Patilla, Aug 2026](https://lapatilla.com/2026/08/10/reduccion-de-la-brecha-cambiaria-en-venezuela-exige-alto-costo-financiero-e-inflacionario/); [El Ucabista, May 2026](https://elucabista.com/2026/05/25/economista-jesus-palacios-proyecta-inflacion-de-192-en-bolivares-y-de-20-en-dolares-al-cierre-de-2026/)
- Palacios projects about 20% inflation measured in USD for 2026. In other words, dollar prices are also rising. — [El Ucabista](https://elucabista.com/2026/05/25/economista-jesus-palacios-proyecta-inflacion-de-192-en-bolivares-y-de-20-en-dolares-al-cierre-de-2026/)
- Economists projected about 10% GDP growth for 2026 (February estimate). — [El Ucabista, Feb 2026](https://elucabista.com/2026/02/13/economistas-proyectan-crecimiento-de-10-del-pib-venezolano-e-inflacion-de-174-en-2026/)
- Official rate about 876.8 Bs/USD on Oct 13, 2026, with the bolívar down about 349% year over year. This comes from a search-result summary and the exact primary page is unverified. — [BCV](https://www.bcv.org.ve/)

**Wages and purchasing power**
- Fedecámaras Q1 2026: average private salary $283/month. Workers earn $223, professionals $471, managers $806. In commerce, workers earn $203. — [Analítica](http://www.analitica.com/actualidad/actualidad-nacional/fedecamaras-sector-privado-reporta-salario-promedio-de-283-y-fuerte-impacto-por-apagones/); [El Nacional](https://www.elnacional.com/2026/06/salario-privado-promedio-venezuela-encuesta-fedecamaras/)

**Service prices (2024 data)**
- Haircut in central Caracas: $8-10. Western Caracas (Catia, Propatria): $5-8. Blow-dry and straightening up to $20. Beard design adds $3-5. Full packages in central and eastern Caracas: $15-30. — [El Diario, Jan 2024](https://eldiario.com/2024/01/23/precios-servicios-de-peluqueria-zonas-de-caracas/)
- Barbershop (Caracas): beard wash, shave and trim $10; with a haircut, $20. — [El Diario, Sep 2024](https://eldiario.com/2024/09/02/cuanto-cuesta-mantener-barba-caracas/)

**Willingness to pay (indirect)**
- ReservaSimple, a booking tool marketed to Venezuela, charges $13.99/month for its Premium plan with WhatsApp reminders and has a free tier of 30 bookings/month. This is the only concrete local price point I found. — [ReservaSimple](https://www.reservasimple.com/agendador-citas-online-venezuela)

### Inferences
- A salon charging $8 per haircut would need about 2-4 extra haircuts a month to justify a $15-30/month subscription. That is plausible for busy multi-chair salons and clinics, but hard for solo or informal barbers, whose monthly income may be only around $200-400.
- Pricing would probably have to be USD-denominated and low ($10-20/month), with a free tier, to compete with ReservaSimple and Turnito.
- The macro picture is improving (opening, growth, narrowing gap) but remains volatile. Inflation is still in the hundreds of percent and rationing hits commerce.

### Gaps
- No 2025-2026 haircut or manicure prices in USD (only 2024). No manicure price data at all. No survey on SMB willingness to pay for software. Independent figures for the parallel exchange rate in October 2026 are unconfirmed.

## 4. Payments: how a foreign SaaS can collect monthly subscriptions

### Takeaway
Card-based recurring billing is the hard part:
- **Stripe** is not available to Venezuelan merchants. A foreign SaaS could bill Venezuelan cards through its own Stripe account (for example in the US or Mexico), but Venezuelan cards reportedly often fail internationally.
- **PayPal** is restricted in Venezuela.
- **What people actually use for USD:** Zelle (only for people with US bank accounts), Binance and USDT, and cash. Pago Móvil covers bolívar payments, but a foreign company cannot receive it without a local entity and bank account.
- **Sanctions:** OFAC relief in 2026 has mostly come through targeted general licenses (oil, banking, contracts), not a full lifting. Compliance screening is still required.

### Cited Findings
- Stripe's global availability list (51 countries) does not include Venezuela or Panama. Mexico is listed. — [Stripe Global](https://stripe.com/global)
- A Venezuelan entrepreneur guide says Stripe does not operate in Venezuela. It also says most Venezuelan USD cards are blocked for international transactions on platforms like Stripe, citing OFAC-linked restrictions. PayPal is described as "limited" for clients inside Venezuela. This is a vendor blog, not an official source. — [VerCatálogo](https://vercatalogo.com/blog/cobrar-en-dolares-en-venezuela)
- PayPal reportedly works in a very restricted way in Venezuela, often allowing sending but not receiving. A 2021 article contradicts this and says dollar receipts were allowed. I found no 2026 source. — [El Diario (PayPal tag)](https://eldiario.com/etiqueta/paypal/); [El Diario 2021](https://eldiario.com/2021/06/17/pagar-con-paypal-en-venezuela/)
- Zelle is widely used for dollar transactions between clients with US bank accounts. Risks include personal accounts being frozen for commercial use and fake payment screenshots. — [Tiendli](https://tiendli.com/blog/zelle-binance-cobrar-venezuela)
- Binance internal transfers of USDT are instant and free. Binance P2P is the usual on- and off-ramp from bolívars (via Pago Móvil) and carries scam risk. — [Tiendli](https://tiendli.com/blog/zelle-binance-cobrar-venezuela); [Fintech Venezuela Guía](https://www.fintechvenezuelaguia.com.ve/2026/02/binance-p2p-como-comprar-dolares-de-forma-segura.html)
- Since about Oct 15, 2026, Binance reportedly allows buying USDT with Pago Móvil and bank transfer without going through P2P, via an exchange house (Insular). This comes from secondary press only. — [Última Hora Venezuela](https://www.ultimahoravenezuela.com/2026/10/binance-habilita-pago-movil-venezuela-usdt.html); [Criptoinforme](https://criptoinforme.com/tutoriales/comprar-usdt-venezuela/)
- Payment guides recommend Pago Móvil (with a QR code), cash in USD and, where possible, a POS terminal as the basics for small merchants. — [Sistemas 4S](https://sistemas4s.com/medios-de-pago-en-venezuela-2026-guia-completa-para-empresas-y-comercios/)

**OFAC general licenses in 2026**
- GL 46 (Jan 29, 2026; later versions 46B, 46C and 46D) authorizes certain transactions involving Venezuelan-origin oil.
- GL 56 and GL 57 (Apr 14, 2026) authorize negotiation of contingent contracts with the Government of Venezuela and certain financial services involving named Venezuelan banks.
- GL 48D covers the supply of certain goods and services to Venezuela (dated Sep 28, 2026).
- GL 60 covers earthquake relief.

The program has shifted from company-specific licenses to category-wide general licenses, but these can be amended or revoked. — [Thompson Hine, Apr 2026](https://www.thompsonhinesmartrade.com/2026/04/ofac-issues-general-licenses-authorizing-venezuelan-certain-commercial-and-banking-transactions/); [Baker McKenzie](https://sanctionsnews.bakermckenzie.com/ofac-issues-new-venezuela-related-general-licenses-authorizing-commercial-negotiations-of-contingent-contracts-with-gov-and-financial-services-involving-named-venezuelan-banks/); [Foley, Aug 2026](https://www.foley.com/insights/publications/2026/08/ofac-reaffirms-recent-licenses-along-with-new-license-in-response-to-venezuela-earthquakes-2/); [Mayer Brown, Jan 2026](https://www.mayerbrown.com/en/insights/publications/2026/01/ofac-issues-new-general-license-authorizing-established-us-companies-to-engage-in-trade-of-venezuelan-oil-and-related-activities)
- Stripe's PayPal subscription mode is available only to European businesses. — [Stripe Docs](https://docs.stripe.com/billing/subscriptions/paypal-subscription-mode?locale=es-419)

### Inferences
- **Realistic collection paths:**
  - Prepaid plans (monthly or quarterly) paid in USDT through Binance Pay, or by Zelle to a US account.
  - A local reseller or partner who collects via Pago Móvil and remits.
  - Card billing through a foreign Stripe account, accepting high decline rates.
- All of these are manual or semi-manual. Churn and collection work would be high, unlike the automatic card renewals possible in Israel or Mexico.
- **Sanctions risk:** sales to private Venezuelan SMBs are not broadly prohibited (the program targets the government, PDVSA and listed persons), but a SaaS must screen against the SDN list and avoid government-linked entities. Payment processors may still de-risk Venezuelan transactions. This is my reading of the program structure and should be confirmed with counsel.
- **Israeli processor:** Tranzila (already integrated in this project) is Israeli and unlikely to support Venezuelan cardholders. This is unverified.

### Gaps
- PayPal's official 2026 policy for Venezuela was not found. No data on Venezuelan card acceptance rates on foreign Stripe accounts. No confirmation of whether Binance Pay merchant (business) accounts are available to a foreign SaaS for recurring collection. I did not get a legal opinion on whether selling SaaS to private Venezuelan businesses needs any OFAC license.

## 5. Local competitors and tools already in use

### Takeaway
Several Spanish-language booking SaaS products already market to Venezuela with WhatsApp reminders, at low prices and with free tiers:
- **ReservaSimple:** $13.99/month premium plan.
- **Turnito:** free plan, with paid tiers that include WhatsApp reminder quotas.
- **AgendaPro:** has a Venezuelan marketplace with listings.
- **Fresha:** has Caracas listings.
- **Others:** DunaSoft, Apúntalo.

None appears to offer a true AI conversational WhatsApp bot plus phone IVR, which is the product's differentiator. Their presence shows that low-cost substitutes exist.

### Cited Findings
- ReservaSimple has Venezuela-specific landing pages for booking and for barbershops. Its free plan allows 30 bookings/month with email notifications. WhatsApp reminders require Premium at $13.99/month. It supports multiple calendars per account. — [ReservaSimple Venezuela](https://www.reservasimple.com/agendador-citas-online-venezuela); [ReservaSimple barberías](https://reservasimple.com/app-citas-barberia-venezuela)
- Turnito has a free plan with a booking link for Instagram and WhatsApp. Paid plans include 100 (Advance) or 250 (Pro) WhatsApp reminders per month. — [Turnito](https://turnito.app/app-turnos-peluqueria/)
- AgendaPro runs a Venezuelan marketplace (agendapro.com/mp/ve/...). One example is a beard-service listing in Lara (Barquisimeto). — [AgendaPro VE listing](https://agendapro.com/mp/ve/barba-lara); [AgendaPro](https://agendapro.com/es)
- Fresha has a Caracas barbershop landing page with listings priced in bolívars. — [Fresha Caracas](https://www.fresha.com/lp/fr/bt/barbiers/ve-caracas)
- DunaSoft Booking (beauty sector, free agenda, SMS and WhatsApp reminders) and Apúntalo (WhatsApp confirmations) also target salons in Spanish. Venezuela availability was not confirmed. — [DunaSoft](https://www.dunasoftpc.com/soluciones/booking-dunasoft/); [Apúntalo](https://apuntalo.com/software-para-peluquerias/)

### Inferences
- The market is not empty, but competitors are mostly "booking link plus reminder" tools. An AI bot that books inside the WhatsApp chat (including voice notes) would be a real differentiator.
- Competitors anchor the price at free to $14/month, which limits pricing power.

### Gaps
- No data on how many Venezuelan businesses use AgendaPro, Fresha or ReservaSimple. No Venezuelan-founded booking startup was identified. Medical and dental scheduling tools (for example Doctoralia's presence in Venezuela) were not researched.

## 6. Connectivity and electricity reliability

### Takeaway
Electricity is the biggest operational risk. 2026 has been the worst year of rationing since 2007:
- Cuts of up to 8 hours a day, in some areas more than 20 hours.
- Nearly all states affected, now including Caracas.
- Industry has lost 44% of its productive hours.

A cloud bot hosted abroad would keep running. But businesses and clients lose power and phone charge, mobile networks degrade during outages, and owners may not see the panel or notifications.

### Cited Findings
- August 2026: rationing of up to 8 hours a day in practically all states, worse in the interior. In Caracas, mall hours and public-employee shifts are limited and residential areas lose power several days a week with no published schedule. Cuts cost industry 44% of its productive hours in 2026. — [El Nacional, Aug 26, 2026](https://www.elnacional.com/2026/08/un-nuevo-racionamiento-electrico-frena-la-economia-y-la-vida-en-venezuela/)
- A specialist calls 2026 the year of greatest rationing since 2007, with the crisis worsening over the first 10 months. Outages of more than 20 hours are a leading cause of over 4,000 protests in 2026 (Observatorio Venezolano de Conflictividad Social). — [Diario Las Américas](https://www.diariolasamericas.com/america-latina/crisis-energetica-seria-gran-obstaculo-acuerdo-petrolero-washington-caracas-n5404142)
- Thermoelectric plants produce only about 2,700 MW of 21,000 MW installed, about 10% of capacity. — [El Nacional, Aug 2026](https://www.elnacional.com/2026/08/termoelectricas-venezuela-apagones-capacidad-instalada-corpoelec-2026/)
- Additional rationing was announced in March 2026. — [El Nacional, May 2026](https://www.elnacional.com/2026/05/racionamientos-la-pesadilla-en-una-venezuela-que-urge-recuperar-su-red-electrica/); [Artículo 66, May 2026](https://www.articulo66.com/2026/05/15/venezolanos-crisis-electrica-apagones-chavismo/)
- Fedecámaras reports a strong impact on business from blackouts. — [Analítica](http://www.analitica.com/actualidad/actualidad-nacional/fedecamaras-sector-privado-reporta-salario-promedio-de-283-y-fuerte-impacto-por-apagones/)
- A doublet earthquake (M7.2 and M7.5) near Morón on Jun 24, 2026 killed at least 2,295 people. OFAC issued GL 60 for relief. — [Wikipedia: 2026 in Venezuela](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_in_Venezuela); [Foley, Aug 2026](https://www.foley.com/insights/publications/2026/08/ofac-reaffirms-recent-licenses-along-with-new-license-in-response-to-venezuela-earthquakes-2/)
- Positive signals: median mobile speed rose 86% year over year to 25.4 Mbps (August 2025). Starlink was licensed on Oct 9, 2026. — [DataReportal Digital 2026](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2026-venezuela); [Wikipedia: 2026 in Venezuela](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_in_Venezuela)

### Inferences
- **Hosting:** servers abroad (Firebase, Railway) are unaffected, but the bot's own WhatsApp line must be hosted outside Venezuela. A Baileys session tied to a phone in Venezuela would be fragile.
- **Outages at the salon:** during a power cut, salons may close without notice. That creates no-shows and cancellations the bot cannot know about unless the owner blocks time. The "maintenance mode" and blocked-time features would be used often.

### Gaps
- No 2026 data on mobile network or internet outages specifically (for example NetBlocks reports or CANTV and Movilnet failures during blackouts).

## 7. Legal and regulatory constraints on customer and patient data

### Takeaway
Venezuela has no comprehensive data-protection law and no data-protection authority. Protection rests on Article 28 (habeas data) and Article 60 (privacy) of the 1999 Constitution, plus Supreme Court case law (2001; ruling 1318 of 2011). The regulatory burden is low, but enforcement is weak and recent breaches undermine trust. Medical data has no specific known framework in the sources found.

### Cited Findings
- There is no general data-protection law and no dedicated authority. Habeas data comes from Constitution Article 28, with privacy under Article 60. — [DataGuidance](https://www.dataguidance.com/taxonomy/term/176); [Global Compliance News](https://www.globalcompliancenews.com/data-privacy/data-protection-enforcement-in-venezuela/)
- A 2001 Supreme Court decision set out the habeas data procedure. Ruling 1318 (2011) was the first to discuss Article 28 privacy principles. — [Global Compliance News](https://www.globalcompliancenews.com/data-privacy/data-protection-enforcement-in-venezuela/)
- Regulation is fragmented. Recent breaches (Movistar 2025, the Cashea app in 2026) show weak protection, and Movistar did not notify affected customers. — [IAPP](https://iapp.org/news/a/venezuela-data-breach-highlights-scattered-privacy-regulation); [CyberPeace](https://cyberpeace.org/resources/blogs/venezuela-data-breaches-expose-fragmented-privacy-regulation)
- The Defensoría del Pueblo handles habeas data complaints in the absence of a dedicated authority. — [Global Compliance News](https://www.globalcompliancenews.com/data-privacy/data-protection-enforcement-in-venezuela/)

### Inferences
- Legal compliance cost to enter is low: a privacy policy and good practice would suffice. Clinics may still expect confidentiality assurances. Selling to medical offices would carry reputational rather than regulatory risk.
- Bigger legal and regulatory items are probably SENIAT tax obligations if a local entity is set up, and OFAC screening.

### Gaps
- I did not check whether a data-protection bill is pending in the 2026 National Assembly. No information on medical-records or health-data rules. No information on tax treatment (VAT/IVA, withholding) of foreign digital services sold to Venezuelan businesses.

### Suggested feasibility scoring (researcher's own synthesis, for the report writer)
- **Demand and fit (WhatsApp-centric culture):** strong, about 7/10.
- **Ability to pay:** weak. Average salary $283/month, haircuts about $5-10, free to $14/month competitors. About 3/10.
- **Payment collection for a foreign SaaS:** poor. No Stripe for local merchants, PayPal restricted, collection is manual via Zelle, USDT or a reseller. About 2-3/10.
- **Infrastructure:** poor. Rationing of up to 8 hours a day in 2026. About 3/10.
- **Regulatory:** light on data protection. OFAC is easing but complex. About 5/10.
- **Overall:** about 3-4/10 as a near-term market. Venezuela looks like an opportunistic or diaspora-led secondary market, not a launch market. Revisit if sanctions normalization brings card networks and payment processors back, and if the power grid recovers.
